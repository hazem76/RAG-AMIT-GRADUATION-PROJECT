================================================================================
                    HOW THE AI WORKS
================================================================================

When you ask a question:

1. Question → Converted to vector embedding
2. Vector → Searched against 11 document chunks in database
3. Top 5 matches → Retrieved from ChromaDB
4. Context + History → Sent to Google Gemini AI
5. AI → Generates natural language response
6. Response → Displayed in chat with confidence score
7. Message → Saved to database for history

All happening in 2-5 seconds!
